**Short version:** we do not collect personal data. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts on this website.

## What is stored on your device

Some features remember your choices **locally in your browser** using `localStorage`:

| Key             | Purpose                                |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `sa:favourites` | Products you saved as favourites       |
| `sa:compare`    | Products selected for comparison       |
| `sa:recent`     | Products you viewed recently (last 12) |
| `sa:theme`      | Your light/dark theme choice           |

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or with the "Clear" buttons on the favourites page.

## Server logs

This site is served as static files. The hosting provider may process technical data (such as IP address, time of request and user agent) in short-lived server logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not combine this data with other sources or use it for profiling.

## Links to other websites

Product pages link to vendor websites. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

## Suggestions and corrections

If you suggest a product or report an error through GitHub or email, the information you provide is processed to review your suggestion. Submissions via GitHub are public and subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

## Applicable law

This website is operated in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). For questions, contact us at the address in the [imprint](/imprint/).
