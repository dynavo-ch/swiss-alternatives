<h2 id="footprint">Swiss footprint</h2>

The footprint is shown as five squares on every card. Each square is one independent question, answered with _yes_, _partly_, _no_ or _unknown_:

1. **Headquarters** — Is the operating company headquartered in Switzerland?
2. **Ownership** — Is the company ultimately Swiss-owned?
3. **Development** — Is the product developed in Switzerland?
4. **Hosting** — Is customer data hosted in Switzerland?
5. **Infrastructure** — Is the hosting infrastructure operated by a Swiss-owned provider?

_Partly_ is used for hosting when only part of the data, or only some customers, are served from Switzerland, or when Swiss hosting is an option rather than the default. Infrastructure counts the operator of the servers — the company itself, a Swiss provider, or a foreign cloud — not the building it sits in.

The optional sort order **“Swiss footprint”** adds one point per _yes_ and half a point per _partly_. It is a transparent count, not a quality rating.

<h2 id="badges">Badges</h2>

Badges are computed from the data. Cards show at most three; the product page shows all that apply.
