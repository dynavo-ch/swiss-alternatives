<h2 id="verification">Verification</h2>

Every statement about ownership, development, hosting and infrastructure carries a basis:

- **Verified** — confirmed by an independent or official source such as the commercial register.
- **Vendor claim** — stated by the vendor in its own documentation, website or legal terms.
- **Inferred** — derived by the editors from indirect evidence; the note explains how.

Typical sources are the Swiss commercial register (Zefix) and UID register, vendors’ legal notices, privacy policies, sub-processor lists, data-centre documentation and press releases about acquisitions. Each entry links its sources and shows the date it was last verified. We aim to re-verify every entry at least once a year and whenever a significant change (such as an acquisition) becomes known.

Missing information is shown as **Unknown / not verified**. It is never converted into “No” — especially not for statements about data location or ownership.

<h2 id="ordering">Ordering & independence</h2>

- Lists are sorted alphabetically by default. Other sort orders are labelled and explained.
- There is no editorial score, no “best” label and no paid placement.
- If affiliate links or sponsorships are ever introduced, they will be marked on the entry and will not influence inclusion, classification or ordering.
- “Alternative to” means overlapping functionality, not a one-to-one replacement. Differences are noted where known.

<h2 id="limits">Limitations</h2>

- Swiss jurisdiction is not a privacy guarantee. Swiss providers are subject to Swiss law, including the surveillance act (BÜPF/LSCPT) and court orders, and may be subject to foreign law where they operate abroad.
- Hosting arrangements change. Always confirm current details in the vendor’s contract or data processing agreement.
- The directory is not exhaustive. Absence from the list says nothing about a product’s quality.

<h2 id="corrections">Corrections</h2>

Every product page has a _Report an error_ link. Vendors are welcome to submit corrections with sources; we update entries when the evidence supports it. See [Suggest a product](/suggest/) for how to propose new entries.
