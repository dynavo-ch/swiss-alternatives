cyon is a web hosting provider from Basel, founded in 2003. Its offering covers
shared web hosting with included email and SSL certificates, domain
registration, a higher-performance Webhosting Pro tier, agency servers on which
web agencies can create and resell their own hosting packages, and managed
servers. Customers manage services through the my.cyon control panel; SSH and
developer tooling are available.

All services run on cyon's own servers, storage and network, located in the
data centre of IWB, the publicly owned Basel utility, about one kilometre from
the company's offices. According to the company, no public cloud providers are
used. In 2024 cyon reported around 48,000 customers, 140,000 managed domains
and 50 employees.

The company is owned and led by its founder. In 2025 it converted from a GmbH
into an AG whose statutes commit it to a positive impact on society and the
environment.
