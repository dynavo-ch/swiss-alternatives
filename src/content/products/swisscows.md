Swisscows is a web search engine operated by Swisscows AG in Egnach, a
subsidiary of Hulbee AG; the service started in 2014. It offers web, image,
video and music search and says it does not build user profiles.
Explicit content is filtered out of results. The free version shows ads from
Microsoft Bing and Google, which receive the query and an anonymised IP
address. Swisscows Pro, at CHF 3.17 per month billed annually,
removes ads and lets users promote or hide particular sources.

Swisscows does not publish in detail where its web results come from. It
states that it uses its own index together with a cooperation with the US
search provider Brave. Earlier descriptions, including Wikipedia and a 2015
interview, said that Swisscows used Bing for most web results and its own
index for German-language searches.

According to Swisscows, its servers are its own and are located in a data
centre in the Swiss Alps, without cloud or third-party hosting providers. The
same company also sells a VPN, an email service and the TeleGuard messenger. In
August 2025 a district court opened bankruptcy proceedings against Swisscows
AG; the Thurgau High Court revoked the decision on appeal in September 2025,
and the company remains active in the commercial register.
