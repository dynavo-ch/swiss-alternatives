tosca is an ERP suite developed by dynasoft AG for small and mid-sized
companies in trade, production and services. Its modules cover purchasing,
sales, CRM, warehouse and logistics, production planning, projects, service,
e-commerce and financial accounting with cost accounting and fixed assets.
Payroll is not built into tosca itself: dynasoft integrates and supports Abacus
payroll, and document management comes from ELO through a partner.

The tosca.mobile add-on gives field sales, warehouse and production staff
access from laptops, tablets and mobile data capture devices. AI functions use
several external providers, including Anthropic, Microsoft Azure, OpenAI,
Google Vertex AI and Mistral AI, with DeepL for translations. Customer
references include Läckerli Huus, Kuhn Rikon and IMPAG.

dynasoft implements and operates tosca itself and monitors installations
around the clock. Its website does not state where operated installations are
hosted, and it publishes no prices.
