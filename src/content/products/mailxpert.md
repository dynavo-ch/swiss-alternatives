mailXpert is an email marketing service developed by mailXpert GmbH in Zurich.
It covers newsletter design with templates, contact management and
segmentation, scheduled sending, automation flows, sign-up forms, surveys and
campaign reporting. Connectors exist for Abacus and bexio, other applications
can be linked through Zapier, and an API is available in the larger plans or as
an option. The related service mailomat provides a sending API and SMTP relay
for transactional email from other applications.

mailXpert states that all data is stored in data centres in Switzerland, with a
backup at a second Swiss site, and that its sending servers are certified by
the Certified Senders Alliance (CSA). The company reports ISO/IEC 27001:2022
certification. Besides the hosted service, mailXpert offers installations in
a data centre chosen by the customer, starting at CHF 15,000; in these hybrid
setups outgoing email is still sent through mailXpert's infrastructure.

There is no free plan. The LITE plan costs CHF 1,590 per year for 240,000
emails and one automation flow, plus a one-time setup fee of CHF 390; larger
plans add more volume, flows and API access. A 30-day free test account is
available. For small organisations, the same company runs Swiss Newsletter, a
lower-priced service with a free tier.
