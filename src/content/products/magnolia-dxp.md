Magnolia DXP is a Java-based content management system and digital experience
platform for large organisations that run many websites, brands, channels and
languages. It combines a visual page editor with headless delivery APIs for
decoupled frontends, and adds digital asset management, personalisation,
analytics and AI features. Connectors are offered for commerce, DAM and
marketing systems such as SAP, Salesforce, commercetools and Cloudinary.

There are two commercial editions. DX Core is installed on the customer's own
infrastructure, on premises or in a private cloud. DX Cloud is a managed
platform-as-a-service that Magnolia operates on the cloud the customer selects,
including hyperscalers and Swiss and European providers, so Swiss hosting is an
option rather than the default. Magnolia states that it is certified to
ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type 2. An open-source Community Edition under GPLv3
continues to be released.

Magnolia was founded in Basel in 1997, released its first open-source version
in 2003 and has been majority-owned by the German investment company GENUI since 2022. Prices start at EUR 3,000 per month for DX Core and EUR 5,000 per month
for DX Cloud.
