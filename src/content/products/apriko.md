Apriko is software for personnel service providers such as staff-leasing
companies and recruitment agencies. It covers candidate and customer
management, vacancies and placements, time recording and absences, payroll
with daily wage calculation and withholding tax codes, billing and margin
analysis. Further functions include credit checks through Dun & Bradstreet,
job advertising on platforms such as LinkedIn and jobs.ch, SMS and email from
within the software, and an open API.

The service runs in the browser as SaaS. Apriko states that it is developed
and hosted in Switzerland but does not name its hosting provider. Users can
log in with Microsoft, Google or Apple accounts and add optional two-factor
authentication. A Swissdec connection for electronic payroll and accident
reporting is listed as planned, not as available.

Apriko AG in Zurich also offers back-office services for agencies, including
payroll, accounts payable and receivable, and financial statements. Prices are
calculated per user licence, but amounts are not published.
