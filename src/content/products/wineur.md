WinEUR, formerly called TOP MAN, is modular accounting and business management
software for small and medium-sized businesses, fiduciaries, foundations and
associations. The core is a multi-company, multi-currency and multilingual
general ledger, extended by modules for receivables and payables, cost
accounting, budgets and reporting, fixed assets, consolidation, stock,
invoicing with quotes and orders, document archiving, e-banking with ISO 20022
payments and QR-bill import, and payroll certified under Swissdec. Mobile apps
record expenses with OCR, time sheets and QR-bills.

WinEUR is sold in three editions, for one company, up to three or an
unlimited number, and can be installed locally or used in GIT's cloud service.
GIT states that cloud data is kept in Swiss data centres with backups in a
second Swiss site, but does not name the operators. Prices are not published;
a free trial and demos are offered.

GIT was founded in Geneva in 1981 and states that all its software is produced
at its Geneva offices. It also sells the payroll software WinSAL and
Peppermint for fiduciaries.
