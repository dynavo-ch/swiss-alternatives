AlpHosting is the hosting brand of TiZoo, a small company founded in 2002 and
based in Crissier near Lausanne. It offers shared hosting with cPanel for PHP,
MySQL and PostgreSQL, Java hosting with Tomcat and other servers, WordPress
hosting, domain registration, SSL certificates, Zimbra-based business email
(AlpMail), backup storage, virtual private servers and dedicated servers.

According to the company, its servers are hosted at BrainServe in Crissier, and
website files and backups stay in Switzerland. An optional GeoHosting service
places copies of a website on servers in other countries, including the United
States, Russia and Hong Kong.

Shared hosting is billed yearly and starts at CHF 55 per year including VAT,
with one domain included and a 30-day money-back guarantee. VPS and dedicated
servers are configured individually and can be billed monthly, half-yearly or
yearly, optionally with server management by AlpHosting.
