Abacus is an ERP and business software suite developed by Abacus Research AG in
Wittenbach SG since 1985. Its modules cover financial accounting, accounts
receivable and payable, fixed assets, electronic banking, HR, payroll, time
and expense management, project management, CRM, ticketing, archiving,
purchasing, production planning, inventory, sales and EDI, plus industry
solutions and AI features. Abacus reports around 65,000 customer companies.

The software is licensed for on-premises operation or used as a hosted service.
Hosting is provided by the group company Abacus Hosting AG, which runs
installations on servers in Swiss data centres, and by Abacus partners.
Implementation, licensing and support are largely handled through a network of
partners; at the end of 2025 Abacus sold its subsidiary Abacus Services to the
partner Axept Business Software.

Abacus does not publish prices. According to Abacus, costs depend on the scope
of the project, and quotes are provided by partners. The group's main market is
Switzerland; it also has subsidiaries in Germany (Munich, Hamburg and Waiblingen).
