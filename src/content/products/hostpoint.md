Hostpoint is a web hosting provider based in Rapperswil-Jona. Its portfolio
includes shared web hosting with WordPress and other one-click applications,
domain registration, email and Cloud Office mailboxes, a website builder,
webshops and managed servers. In 2013 Hostpoint became the first Swiss domain
registrar accredited by ICANN; it reports managing more than 1.3 million
domains.

Customer web, email and database data is stored on Hostpoint's own servers in
the Digital Realty data centre in Glattbrugg near Zurich. The facility operator
is a US-owned company. Hostpoint states that some components of the integrated
SEO tool rankingCoach are the only exception to storage in Switzerland.

Prices are listed in CHF including VAT, and web hosting can be tested for 30 days
free of charge. The website and support are available in German, French, Italian
and English.
