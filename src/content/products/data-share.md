data.share is a browser-based document management system organised as virtual
data rooms, offered by DATEN PARTNER GmbH in Zurich. Rooms hold documents with
metadata and categories, version history, role- and user-based permissions,
time-limited external share links, rule-based routing and workflows, and a log
of all changes. Two-factor authentication is available, and the interface
follows the browser language in German, English, French or Italian. There are
no native mobile apps.

Daten Partner packages the platform for specific uses: dsProperty for building
documentation including BIM models, dsFoundation for foundations, dsArt for art
collections, dsLegacy for personal and business succession documents, and
dsExchange for small offices and associations. It connects to Microsoft
SharePoint and Skribble. The vendor reports about 54,000 data.share users and
3,800 project rooms opened since 2001.

According to Daten Partner, all servers are in Zurich and the software is
developed in Switzerland; the data-centre operator is not named. Prices are
quoted individually, and a 30-day trial is offered.
