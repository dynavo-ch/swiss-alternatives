Sherpany is a meeting management platform for boards of directors, executive
committees and other formal leadership meetings. It covers agenda building,
distribution of meeting documents (available online and offline), private
notes, minutes with approval workflows, digital circular resolutions and
voting, and tracking of tasks and decisions. It integrates with Microsoft 365,
Outlook and Teams and offers e-signing for approved minutes. It is listed under
document management because its core is the secure distribution and archiving
of confidential meeting material.

The service is hosted in Swiss data centres, and Sherpany states that it holds
ISO/IEC 27001 certification and an ISAE 3000 Type II report and is audited for
compliance with FINMA circular 2018/3 on outsourcing. Organisations can choose
SaaS or a hybrid-cloud setup with document storage on their own premises.
Single sign-on via SAML, SCIM provisioning, two-factor authentication and
remote wiping are available.

Sherpany was founded in Zurich in 2011 and has been owned by the US-based
Datasite group since 2024, which in turn is majority-owned by the London-based
private equity firm CapVest. Prices are not published.
