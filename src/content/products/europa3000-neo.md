europa3000 neo is an ERP system for small and medium-sized Swiss companies. Its
base modules cover addresses, items, the order process from quote to delivery
note, documents, receivables and payables, general ledger with balance sheet
and income statement, dunning and payments; industry modules extend it for
trade, production, service companies, non-profit organisations and fiduciary
firms. The current version adds a redesigned interface, a built-in AI assistant
called EURA that analyses financial data, and MEMORA for tasks and notes linked
to customers, orders and items.

The software is licensed as a subscription and implemented through a network of
certified partners. It runs on the customer's own servers or, as ERPaaS, in the
Swiss data centre of CONVOTIS Schweiz AG, accessed through a browser and Citrix.
Prices are not published.

europa3000 has built ERP software in Aarau for more than 35 years. Since January
2026 it has been part of the SelectLine Group, whose holding company is based in
Magdeburg, Germany.
