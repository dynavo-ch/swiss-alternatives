Winbiz Cloud is browser-based business software for small Swiss companies,
self-employed people, start-ups and their fiduciaries. One
package covers invoicing with QR-bills, supplier payments, double-entry
accounting with VAT, automated bank reconciliation, payroll certified to
Swissdec 5, and project management with time recording. Integrated e-banking
and e-invoicing, unlimited addresses, items and currencies, mobile apps and an
app store with add-ons from third parties are included.

Winbiz Cloud is priced per access from CHF 64 per month excluding VAT with
annual billing; a locally installed edition, Winbiz Local, is still sold.
Support is provided by a team in Switzerland.

The software goes back to La Gestion Electronique in Martigny, which the French
Fiducial group took over in 2013. The publisher, Fiducial Winbiz, is
registered in Geneva. Its terms name a Zurich cloud provider that has since
merged into CONVOTIS Schweiz AG as the host.
