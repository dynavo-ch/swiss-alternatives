Ticinocom, known by its ticino.com domain, is an internet service provider in
Muralto that has operated since 1996. Alongside fibre, mobile, telephony and TV
services, it offers shared web hosting, WordPress hosting with automatic
installation and updates, email, SSL certificates and registration of .ch, .li
and other domains, plus a free website builder with basic e-commerce features
for hosting customers.

Ticinocom states that hosted data is kept on servers it owns in data centres in
Ticino, in a building certified to SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, and that the
electricity used is matched by hydropower from Ticino. The building operator is
not named.

Plans are billed monthly in CHF, starting at CHF 9.90 per month after a 30-day
free trial; a domain is included with the S, M and L plans. Support is provided
in Italian; the website is also available in German.
