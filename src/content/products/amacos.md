amacos is a reporting app for companies whose staff work on site, such as
service and repair technicians, trades, agricultural contractors, facility
services and transport companies. Employees record working hours, materials,
machine hours, work reports and photos on a tablet or smartphone, also offline,
and send them to the office. There the data flows into a desktop business
application with order intake, resource planning and dispatch, post-calculation
and invoicing, and can be passed on to financial accounting. Reporting forms
are modelled on each customer's existing paper forms.

According to the privacy policy, data is stored on Martha Software's own servers
in a data centre in Switzerland, which is not named, and the app transfers data
over HTTPS. The company lists ISO 9001 among its certifications on the amacos
website.

Prices depend on the selected modules and are quoted on request. Martha-Software
GmbH in Gipf-Oberfrick (AG) also develops software for pig breeding (Pimatec)
and livestock trading (Herdia). Since 2025 it has belonged to beeSnap GmbH of
Freienbach (SZ), whose owner Daniel Santos is CEO of Martha Software.
