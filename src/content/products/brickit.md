brickit is browser-based business software for Swiss trades businesses; its
time tracking is recommended by the Swiss painters' association SMGV. It covers
customer and property records, quotes from a service catalogue, orders, crew and equipment
scheduling with a six-month capacity preview, time and expense recording per
job, cost tracking with receipts, post-calculation of margins, and invoicing
from advance to final invoices. Field staff see their assignments and record
time on a smartphone or tablet.

Accounting is connected through an interface to bexio, and data, including
attachments, can be exported as Excel or CSV. Import of NPK bills of
quantities is planned. brickit states that business data is stored in
Switzerland but does not name its hosting provider.

There are two licences, priced per user and billed annually: an office licence
at CHF 69.90 and a site licence at CHF 9.90 per month, excluding VAT. The
company was founded in 2023 in the canton of Zug.
