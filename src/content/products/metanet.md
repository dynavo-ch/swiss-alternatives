METANET is a web hosting provider based in Zurich. It offers domain
registration, email and shared web hosting (METAhost S, M and L) with a website
builder, WordPress hosting, virtual servers, managed services and security
services. The company reports more than 60,000 customers and over 1,000 server
systems.

METANET's servers are housed in rented racks at Equinix, GTT and SenseLAN data
centres in Zurich, Oberengstringen and Düdingen, connected to its own network
(AS21069). The ISO 27001 certificates METANET publishes are those of its
data-centre partners Equinix and GTT, not of METANET itself.

Since 2022 METANET has been part of the dogado group, which merged with one.com
to form group.one; the group's parent company is registered in Sweden. Prices
are listed in CHF including VAT, with a minimum term of 12 months for web
hosting.
