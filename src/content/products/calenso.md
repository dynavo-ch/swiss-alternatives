Calenso is an online booking platform that lets customers book appointments
with an organisation's staff through widgets in the organisation's own design.
It covers room and resource booking, video and phone meetings, questionnaires
before the appointment, automated reminders and analytics, and connects to
calendars and other business software. Published case studies include health
insurers, a car importer and cantonal administrations.

Calenso says its servers are hosted in Switzerland in an ISO 27001 and BSI C5
certified data centre, while its privacy policy says personal data is stored
primarily in Switzerland and the EU and names AWS as a hosting provider. Prices
are only available on request, so it is not aimed at individuals looking for a
simple personal booking page.

Calenso AG is based in Rothenburg near Lucerne. Since 2025 it has been part of
jrni Holdings, Inc., a US customer-interaction group, and is therefore not
Swiss-owned.
