hosttech is a web hosting provider founded in 2004 in Richterswil on Lake Zurich.
Its portfolio includes shared hosting with Plesk, WordPress hosting, domain
registration, email and Hosted Exchange, SSL certificates, a website builder,
virtual and dedicated servers, managed servers, reseller hosting and colocation.
The company reports more than 60,000 customers.

hosttech operates its own data centres in Switzerland, including DATAROCK, an
underground former Swiss Army hospital in Nottwil (Lucerne) opened in 2018, and DATAPARK in
Wädenswil (Zurich). It states that its data centres are ISO 27001 certified,
that it is PCI DSS certified and that it uses certified renewable electricity.
Separately registered companies of the same name run hosttech's websites for
Germany and Austria.

Prices are listed in CHF including VAT. Web hosting plans run for 12 months,
carry a one-time setup fee and come with a 30-day money-back guarantee; the
website builder can be tested for 30 days free of charge.
