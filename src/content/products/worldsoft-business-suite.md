The Worldsoft Business Suite is a web-based software package from Worldsoft AG
for small businesses. Its CRM manages leads and contacts with categories,
ratings, a dashboard and statistics, and web forms can trigger automatic
follow-up email sequences. It can be extended with email marketing, online
surveys, invoicing, an online shop and an affiliate system, and connects to
Worldsoft's own website builder and to WordPress.

Worldsoft sells the suite together with website, email hosting and domain
packages. Prices are in CHF; the CRM starts at CHF 9.90 per month and the
Business Solutions packages with shop and invoicing are listed at CHF 24.90 to
CHF 54.90 with a twelve-month contract term; email marketing is included from
the Business package.

Worldsoft's current data processing agreement lists server operators in
Switzerland (EveryWare), France (OVH) and Germany (Hetzner). Most of its
customers are small businesses in Germany, Switzerland and Austria.
