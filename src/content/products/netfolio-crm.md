Netfolio CRM is a customer relationship management tool for asset managers.
It records relationships between contracting parties, beneficial owners and
other contacts, keeps a journal whose entries lock after a set period, and
manages tasks and recurring workflows. KYC data is captured by person type with
a risk assessment for higher-risk business relationships, and documents can be
uploaded manually or imported through bank interfaces. Reports support
regulatory audits.

The CRM can be used on its own or together with the other Netfolio modules
from Alphasys, including a portfolio management system, a MiFIR reporting
interface and a reporting module. Alphasys is a founding member of the
OpenWealth Association and connects banks through the OpenWealth standard and
the bLink platform of SIX.

Netfolio runs in the browser. Alphasys says the software is operated by its
IT partner exxo entirely in Switzerland, and customers can also host the data
themselves. Prices are not published.
