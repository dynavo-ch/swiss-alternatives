Infomaniak's shared web hosting includes 250 GB of SSD storage and up to 20
websites, unlimited MySQL/MariaDB databases and traffic, free Let's Encrypt
certificates, SSH and cron access, one-click installation of WordPress and other
CMS, and automatic backups with restore up to seven days back. Node.js, MongoDB,
Redis and other technologies require the more expensive Managed Cloud Server.

As an ICANN-accredited registrar, Infomaniak registers domains in more than 500
extensions. Each domain includes DNSSEC, DynDNS, one email address and a
one-page website; domain privacy is listed as a separate option. Prices are
published in CHF excluding VAT.

Hosting, development and support are based in Switzerland, where Infomaniak runs
its own data centres. A credit card is required to start the 30-day free trial
of web hosting.
