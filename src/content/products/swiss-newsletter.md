Swiss Newsletter is a newsletter service operated by mailXpert GmbH in Zurich
and launched in 2020 for micro-businesses, start-ups and associations. It
offers an email editor with templates, contact management with custom fields
and segments, scheduled sending, personalised content blocks, sign-up forms
and live reporting with PDF export. Emails can also be sent in French, Italian
and English as a paid add-on, and a survey module is available with yearly
billing.

The service runs on the same platform as mailXpert. The vendor states that
customer address data stays in Switzerland, and its sending servers are
certified by the Certified Senders Alliance (CSA). Customers who need more
volume or email automation can convert their subscription to a mailXpert plan
and keep their data.

The free version allows up to 200 emails per month. Paid subscriptions are
priced by monthly volume, starting at CHF 20 per month for 2,000 emails
(excluding VAT), with a 10% discount for yearly payment. Payment is possible
by card, TWINT or invoice.
