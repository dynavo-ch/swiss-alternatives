Nova Smart NG is an integrated HR and payroll system. Its modules cover
payroll, absence management, time and attendance, planning, recruitment,
training, evaluations, expense claims, electronic documents and HR analytics,
with a self-service portal and mobile apps for iOS and Android. The platform
handles several legal entities, countries and currencies, and Novative says it
has more than 600 clients in over 30 countries in Europe, Africa and the USA.

For Switzerland, the payroll is listed by Swissdec as certified (basic level,
ELM 5.0 to 5.3). Novative also describes an information security management
system based on ISO 27001; its website does not say clearly whether a current
certificate exists, so no certification is recorded here.

Novative SA in Nyon also offers HR and payroll outsourcing and employer of
record services. The software is described as hosted in a Swisscom data centre
in Switzerland. Prices are only available after a demo.
