Fabasoft OneGov is a records and case management system (GEVER) for Swiss
cantons, cities, municipalities and organisations close to the public sector,
such as associations, church communities and universities. It covers digital
files and dossiers, processes from co-reporting procedures to council
decisions, a BPMN 2.0 editor for workflows, versioning with a full audit trail,
archiving, and AI features that triage incoming mail, assign documents and
translate them. Login is supported through AGOV and the Swiss e-ID.

The product continues the OneGov GEVER solution of the Bern company
4teamwork, which merged with Fabasoft's Swiss subsidiary in 2022. Fabasoft
states that OneGov is developed in Bern and runs on its PROCECO cloud platform
in two redundant Swiss data centres, with attestations including BSI C5, ISO
27001 and 27018, ISAE 3402 Type 2 and the EU Cloud Code of Conduct.

Fabasoft Schweiz AG is majority-owned by Fabasoft AG in Linz, Austria. Prices
are not published.
