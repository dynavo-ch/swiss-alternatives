ORIA is the cloud and managed services brand of Sharp DX (Schweiz) AG, the
Rotkreuz IT service provider known until August 2026 as ITpoint. Its Enterprise
Cloud is a geo-redundant infrastructure platform in two Swiss data centres on
which customers obtain virtual servers, storage, backup and disaster recovery,
private clouds on dedicated hardware and an IBM Power cloud for applications
built on IBM architecture. Services are put together from a catalogue and can extend to the
complete operation of a customer's IT, with a service desk and Microsoft 365,
modern workplace and managed XDR services on top.

The platform targets regulated companies, including financial firms under
FINMA rules and pharmaceutical manufacturers that need GxP and GAMP 5
qualified infrastructure. Sharp DX states ISO/IEC 20000 and ISO/IEC 27001
certification and an ISAE 3402 Type II report. A customer portal is available;
prices are quoted on request.

The provider has belonged to the Sharp group since 2021 and was renamed Sharp DX
in 2026. It states that the infrastructure is its own, that data stays in
Switzerland and that colleagues at Sharp DX companies abroad are involved only
in selected services.
