InvestGlass is a client relationship management platform aimed at banks,
private banks, wealth managers, brokers, insurers and other regulated
organisations. Besides CRM pipelines and contact management, it combines
digital client onboarding with KYC, OCR and liveness checks, FATCA/CRS and AML
tools, e-signatures, email and SMS campaigns, a client portal and a portfolio
management system with model portfolios and risk monitoring. AI features
suggest next steps to relationship managers.

The platform is offered as a cloud service hosted in Switzerland or, in the
Enterprise plan, as an on-premise installation. InvestGlass presents the
product as a Swiss-hosted alternative to large CRM suites from Salesforce and
Microsoft and connects to other systems through an API.

InvestGlass SA was founded in Geneva in 2014. In a job posting on its website it lists
Switzerland, France, Germany, Austria, Luxembourg and Belgium as its sales
markets. Plans are priced per user and month in CHF. The privacy policy that
names the hosting providers has not been revised since 2019.
