BISON ERP is merchandise management and ERP software sold in industry editions.
BISON Retail ERP covers purchasing, scheduling, warehouse, branch processes,
pricing, promotions, sales and invoicing for food and specialist retailers,
wholesalers, franchise systems and omnichannel businesses with anything from a
few to more than 1,000 branches; a mobile app, electronic shelf labels and a
point-of-sale system are offered alongside. BISON Agrar ERP adds seed
production, seasonal planning and batch tracking for agricultural wholesale,
BISON Energie ERP covers contracts, compulsory stocks and logistics for fuel
traders, and BISON Commodity Trading ERP handles contracts, hedging, logistics
and risk management in international commodity trading.

BISON develops the software itself and provides implementation, support and
operation; customer-specific changes are kept release-compatible, and BISON
states that it works on the standard product every week. Prices are not
published. Customers include LANDI and other fenaco companies as well as
retailers in Germany.

Bison Schweiz AG belongs to fenaco, the Swiss agricultural cooperative, and
since 2025 has also included the group's former internal IT unit. Development takes
place in Switzerland and at its German subsidiary.
