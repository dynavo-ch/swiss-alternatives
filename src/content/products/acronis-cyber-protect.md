Acronis Cyber Protect is backup and endpoint security software for businesses
from Acronis, headquartered in Schaffhausen. It combines image and file-level
backup, immutable backups and continuous data protection with anti-malware,
exploit prevention, vulnerability assessment, patch management and, in the
cloud-managed version, endpoint detection and response. It protects Windows,
macOS and Linux machines, mobile devices, VMware, Hyper-V, Nutanix, Proxmox and
other hypervisors, and Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace data. The Standard,
Advanced and Backup Advanced editions differ in features and supported
workloads; managed service providers use the separate Acronis Cyber Protect
Cloud platform, and consumers the related Acronis True Image.

The product is managed either through the Acronis cloud console or through a
management server installed on the customer's premises, with backups stored
locally, in the Acronis cloud or both. Acronis operates its own data centres
and also uses Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure; customers choose the region.
Acronis opened a Swiss data-centre location in 2016, but its current public
pages do not list data-centre locations, so present availability in Switzerland
could not be confirmed.

Acronis was founded in Singapore in 2003 and has its research and development
centres in Bulgaria, the United States, Israel and Singapore. The Swedish
investor EQT has been its majority owner since 2025. In September 2025 the US
Director of National Intelligence issued an order under the Federal Acquisition
Supply Chain Security Act excluding Acronis products and services from US
intelligence community systems; the reasons were not published.
