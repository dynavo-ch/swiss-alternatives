ProTimer is a cloud-based time and attendance system for small and
medium-sized businesses in Switzerland. It records working hours, breaks,
overtime and night work, manages holidays and absences with approval
workflows, and covers expenses and shift planning. Industry versions implement
the collective labour agreements of restaurants (L-GAV), petrol station shops,
bakeries, painters and plasterers and timber construction, and a garage
version connects to SwissGarage.

Employees clock in on touch, fingerprint or badge terminals that ProTimer
designs and assembles itself, or through apps for iOS, Android and Windows;
administrators work in the browser on PC, Mac or tablet. Optional modules add
project and service recording and a payroll module that can also be used on
its own. Payroll data can be exported to Abacus, SwissSalary, Topal and Mirus.

The software has been developed since 1997, and ProTimer GmbH was founded in 2001. In August 2025 the company was sold to Vierwald Software AG in Rotkreuz
as a succession arrangement, with the founder remaining managing director.
Prices are published in CHF excluding VAT and depend on the number of
employees.
