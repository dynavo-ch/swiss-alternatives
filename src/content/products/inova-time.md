inova:time is a modular time tracking service for small companies, SMEs,
service providers and public administrations in Switzerland. The working-time
module records hours, overtime, holidays and absences according to configurable
working-time models, break and overtime rules; further modules add project time
and service recording, expense management and staff planning. Employees book
time in the browser or in a mobile app, and reports and Excel exports are
generated automatically.

The working-time module costs from CHF 6 per user and month, and further
modules from CHF 1.50 each, billed as a modular annual subscription. Project
managers from inova:solutions support the introduction, from requirements
analysis to training.

inova:solutions, based in Ostermundigen near Bern, also develops finance,
cost-accounting and planning software for public administrations. It states
that inova:time is developed and operated in Switzerland and that its data is
stored in a Swiss data centre.
