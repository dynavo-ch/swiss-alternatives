# Code of conduct

This project is a shared, public reference. We want contributing to it to be a
good experience for everyone, whatever their background, language or level of
experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including towards vendors whose products
  are discussed.
- Stay factual. Disagreements about an entry are settled with sources, not
  with volume.
- Disclose conflicts of interest — for example when you work for a vendor whose
  listing you change.
- Give constructive, specific feedback on contributions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately adding false or misleading information, or astroturfing for or
  against a product.
- Spam, advertising or attempts to buy placement outside the published
  [sponsorship policy](https://swiss-alternatives.ch/sponsors/).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently exclude people who violate this code. Report problems privately to
**info@swiss-alternatives.ch**; reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
